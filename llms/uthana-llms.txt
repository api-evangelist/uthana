# Uthana - The fastest way to bring your characters to life

Generative AI for 3D character animation.

>_ Create motion from text, video, or real-time inputs.
>_ Instantly apply to any character, output anywhere.
>_ Works for animators, developers, and studios.

## Generate Motion
> Create motion however you work best — all instantly applied to your characters.

### Describe & animate
Generate lifelike motions from a simple text prompt in seconds. Then tweak, trim, save, and download.

- Learn more about text to motion at https://uthana.com/product/text-to-motion

### Animate from 2D video
Upload any reference video of an action or movement to generate accurate, character-ready motions.

- Learn more about video to motion at https://uthana.com/product/video-to-motion

### Stitch & loop
Join multiple clips into a single seamless, realistic motion — without any keyframe work.

- Learn more about:
  - Stitching at https://uthana.com/product/stitching
  - Looping at https://uthana.com/product/looping

### Control in real time
Drive characters live in-engine with generative AI motion — choose from 50+ unique character styles.

- Learn more about real-time animation at https://uthana.com/product/real-time-animation

### Move & record
Control your character’s movement interactively, then save the output as a ready-to-use motion clip.

### Search
Access our library of over 50,000 studio-quality motion clips — with new content added regularly.

## Works with any character, outputs anywhere
> Our proprietary IK retargeting works with any rig, regardless of skeleton structure. Don't have a rig? Uthana can auto-rig in seconds.

- Learn more about flexible retargeting at https://uthana.com/product/flexible-retargeting
